# 0035. AGPL-3.0-or-later

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: Accepted

## Context
ADR 0002 licensed every crate GPL-3.0-or-later. The GPL lets anyone run a modified copy as a network service
without publishing the changes. The author is the only copyright holder of this repository and of the three apps,
so the licence can change for new versions. All third-party crates are permissive (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, ISC,
Zlib, Unicode) or offer such a licence as an option, and `option-ext` is MPL-2.0; all of them combine with the
AGPL. The OpenOrienteering Mapper symbol sets and test maps are GPL-3, and GPLv3 section 13 allows combining them
with an AGPLv3 work.

## Decision
Every crate is AGPL-3.0-or-later. Third-party material keeps its licence and is listed with source and licence in
the crate that carries it; the OpenOrienteering Mapper files stay GPL-3.0. ADR 0002 is superseded.

## Consequences
- Whoever runs a modified version as a network service must offer its source to the users (AGPL section 13).
  Local use of the apps does not change.
- Releases up to v0.27.0 stay GPL-3.0-or-later for those who received them.
- A fork under another name stays allowed, as it was under the GPL.
- Cost if wrong: a user who needs the GPL without the network clause has no licence for new versions; the
  author could grant one, because the author holds all rights.

Source: freemapper ADR 0152; the licence audit of 2026-11-15.
